## Course Overview

The **Intelligent Internet of Things** course provides students with the fundamental operating principles of Internet of Things (IoT) systems, with particular attention to communication protocols, distributed architectures, and design and development principles. The course combines theoretical lectures with hands-on laboratory sessions using a real testbed and IoT devices, and offers insight into the technological and research trends shaping the IoT and Edge Computing landscape.

## Learning Objectives

1. **IoT Operating Principles**:
   - Acquire a solid understanding of communication protocols, distributed architectures, and IoT system design and development principles.
2. **Applied and Hands-On Knowledge**:
   - Gain practical experience through a dedicated testbed and IoT devices in the laboratory.
3. **Technological and Research Trends**:
   - Explore current and emerging trends in IoT and Edge Computing.

## Course Structure

The course combines frontal lectures with laboratory workshops and hands-on exercises. The first part introduces IoT foundations, communication protocols and paradigms, architectural approaches, Web of Things and interoperability topics, and the relationship between IoT, Cloud and Edge Computing. The second part is dedicated to laboratory work and direct experimentation with IoT technologies, operating systems, protocols, programming languages, libraries, and application-level development tools.

The laboratory part concludes with a project (individual or group) involving the design and development of a complete IoT application, followed by a project report. The final grade is a weighted average of the written test and project grades.

The course is delivered in Italian, in-person, with no mandatory attendance. Lessons rely on dedicated slides provided to students, complemented by references to textbooks and additional teaching material. Two seminars are also included, featuring companies operating in the IoT sector and a look at upcoming technological developments.

## 🎓 Main Topics

**(A) IoT Introduction & IoT Networking**
  - IoT Physical / Link Layer
  - IoT Network and Transport Layer
  - TCP & UDP Overview

**(B) IoT Application Layer Protocols & Communication Paradigms**
  - Protocol & Communication Paradigms Overview
  - HTTP RESTful API – Lecture, Hands-On & Laboratory
  - MQTT (MQ Telemetry Transport) – Lecture, Hands-On & Laboratory
  - Constrained Application Protocol (CoAP) – Lecture, Hands-On & Laboratory

**(C) IoT Discoverability & Interoperability**
  - Resource & Service Discovery, CoRE Interfaces & IoT Media Types
  - IoT Smart Object Design – Hands-On Session & Laboratory
  - IoT & Web of Things

**(D) IoT Architecture & Design**
  - IoT & Edge Computing
  - IoT End-to-End Design
  - Monolithic and Microservice Architectures for the IoT
  - IoT Hardware & Software
  - IoT Security & Privacy

**(E) Advanced Applications & IoT Intelligence**
  - Virtual Objects & Digital Twins
  - IoT Data Processing, Visualization & Machine Learning
